Information you provide

We do not operate a user-account system in the website or app. When you contact us by phone, email, WhatsApp, or in person to enquire about or book air tickets, hotels, tours, or cargo services, you may choose to share details such as your name, contact number, email address, travel dates, and travel document information. How bookings are completed

The app loads our website. Booking requests are completed through WhatsApp at +977 9851150530. Messages you send us are handled by our staff and may also be subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. We do not sell your details to third parties.

Information collected automatically

Our website hosting provider may record standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and timestamps for security, reliability, and basic analytics.   • Device PDF viewer — opens our brochure when you tap "Open brochure".

These services may process limited technical data under their own privacy policies. The brochure is a static PDF and is not used to collect personal information.

Data retention and security

Booking-related information is retained only as long as needed to deliver the service, comply with legal or airline requirements, and maintain business records. Access is limited to authorised staff. No payment card details are collected by the website or app.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. A parent or guardian should make any travel enquiry on a child's behalf.

Your choices and contact

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the booking information you shared with us by contacting globalcitytraveltours@gmail.com or calling +977 9851150530. We will respond as soon as reasonably possible.
